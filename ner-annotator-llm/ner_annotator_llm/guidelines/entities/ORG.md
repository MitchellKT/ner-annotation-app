# ORG — An organised body of people: company, institution, authority, team.

## What counts as an ORG entity
- Companies, government bodies, agencies, courts, universities, schools,
  hospitals, political parties, sports teams, bands, NGOs, alliances.
- A department or subsidiary is its own entity when the text treats it as
  one ("the City Council", "the marketing team").
- Governments and capitals used metonymically for the authority
  ("Washington said", "Brussels ruled") are ORG mentions.
- A market, an industry or an unorganised crowd is not an ORG.

## What counts as a mention
- Full names, short forms and acronyms ("the Federal Reserve", "the Fed",
  "NATO"), descriptive references ("the bank", "the company") and pronouns
  referring to the organisation ("it", "they").
- Take the full official name including its legal suffix when present
  ("Acme Corp."), but leave out a preceding article that is not part of
  the name.

## Flags
- `relative`: the organisation is identified only through a relation —
  "her employer", "the company's biggest rival".
- `implicit`: it is named but in a background or modifier role — "a
  SpaceX engineer", "the Google logo".
